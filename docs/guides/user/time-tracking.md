# Time tracking and effort

## Purpose
Log time against tasks, review it, and compare with effort and budget.

## Weekly timesheet
Open **More → Timesheets** (if enabled in your profile sidebar settings).
- Switch weeks with **Previous week**, **This week**, **Next week**.
- **Find a task to pin** → **Pin {task}**; **Unpin {task}** removes a row.
- In a day cell enter **Duration** as `H:MM` or whole minutes (1–1440) and an optional **Comment**. Clear both to delete an entry. Alt+arrow moves between cells. **Total** sums each row/day.
- **Locked** entries cannot be changed. On a conflict: cancel the draft and re-enter.

## Timelog view
In a project add a **Timelog** view. Filters: **From date**, **To date**, **Category**, **Billing type** (**Billable** / **Non-billable**), **User**; **Timelog sort**; **By user**. Shows **Actual hours** and, where allowed, **Actual cost** and “% of budget used”. **Use as a project budget** marks a currency field as budget.

## Rates
Administrators maintain **Hourly rates** (person or job role, **Rate per hour**, **Effective from**). Rates are snapshotted on each entry, so later changes do not alter logged time.

## Effort
See [Dependencies and scheduling](dependencies-and-scheduling.md#working-days-duration-effort). Planned vs actual: **Workload** ([Views](views.md#workload)) and reports ([Reports and dashboards](reports-and-dashboards.md)).

## Permissions
Money columns are hidden for people without cost access (public dashboards mask financial widgets).

## Limitations
No start/stop timer was found in the UI strings; time is entered manually.
