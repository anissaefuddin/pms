# Search and navigation

## Search
Choose **Search** in the sidebar. Type at least two characters in the query box (“Find tasks, projects, comments, or people”).
- **Search scope**: **Everywhere**, **This space**, **This project**.
- **Show**: All, Tasks, Projects & folders, Comments, People. **Show all results** expands a group.
- **Filters**: **Assigned to me**, **Status** (Any, Active, Completed, Deferred, Cancelled). **Sort by** Relevance or Date modified.
Results only include what you may see.

## Other pages (sidebar and **More** menu)
- **My to-do** (**Filter tasks**: All/Only active/Only overdue; **Sort tasks**; change status inline).
- **Starred**: items starred with **Star this item**; private to you.
- **Shared with me**, **File reviews**, **Portfolio**, **Workload**, **Timesheets**, **Dashboards**, **Reports**.
- **Request forms**, **Stream**, **Inbox**.

## Recycle bin, starred, shared with me
**Recycle bin** lists deleted items: **Restore**, **Undo** right after deleting, **Empty Recycle bin** (permanent, only items you may delete; confirm “Delete permanently”). Restore is blocked if the original Space was deleted or you lack permission.

## Help
**Help** menu: **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Documentation**; **Send feedback** in the account menu.
